# Apps for learning English (Mac mini M4 + Pixel 10 Pro)

Verified 2026-10-02. **[U]** = status or price not confirmed. Prices in US$ are from third-party sources; check the Spanish Play Store for euros.
For how these fit together with Claude, see [research/learning-with-claude-and-apps.md](research/learning-with-claude-and-apps.md).

## Exam preparation (official)

| Tool | Platform | Cost | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cambridge Write & Improve | Web | Free; Test Zone £4.50/mo | Instant CEFR feedback on writing. Cross-check Claude's marks here |
| Cambridge Speak & Improve | Web | Free (research project) | AI speaking level estimate, Linguaskill-style |
| Cambridge Test & Train | Web, mobile | Paid via exam centres | Short drills for B2 First / C1 Advanced |
| British Council IELTS Ready | Web | Free tier; Premium free with a BC booking | 40 IELTS mock tests (replaces the old IELTS Prep app) |
| IELTS by IDP | Android, web | Free; prep course 14-day trial | Booking, results, practice |
| Official TOEFL Practice (ETS) | Android | Free sets + premium | Practice for the 2026 TOEFL format (replaces TOEFL Go! **[U]**) |
| Aptis practice (British Council) | Web | Free | Format familiarity |

## Vocabulary

- **Anki** (macOS, free) + **AnkiDroid** (Android, free), synced through a free AnkiWeb account. The core tool.
- **Quizlet** (web, Android): free with ads, Plus ~US$36/yr. Easier, weaker retention.

## Dictionaries and vocabulary level

| Tool | Platform | Cost | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cambridge Dictionary | Web | Free | Learner definitions, UK/US audio, CEFR level shown on many entries |
| Oxford Learner's Dictionaries | Web | Free | Definitions + the **Oxford 3000/5000** word lists tagged by CEFR level: a ready-made B2/C1 study list |
| English Vocabulary Profile (Cambridge) | Web | Free **[U: may need registration]** | Shows which *meaning* of a word is B2 vs C1. Use it to aim your vocabulary at your exam level |
| Ozdic | Web | Free | Collocations dictionary ("make a decision", "heavy rain"). Key for Use of English |
| Text Inspector | Web | Freemium **[U]** | Paste an essay to see the CEFR level of the vocabulary you used |

## Input: listening and reading

- **Language Reactor** (Chrome on the Mac): free, Pro ~US$40/yr. Dual subtitles on Netflix/YouTube. Desktop only.
- **YouGlish** (web, Android): free. Hear any word in real clips, filter by accent.
- **BBC Learning English**: the app closed in Dec 2023. Use the website, podcasts ("6 Minute English") and YouTube.
- **Readlang** (web, extension): free tier. Tap-to-translate on web articles.
- **LingQ** (web, Android): ~US$120/yr. Reading and listening with imported content.
- **Kindle for Android** with Word Wise: free with Kindle books **[U]**.

## Speaking and pronunciation

| Tool | Platform | Cost | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude voice mode | Android, Mac | Free (Haiku) / Pro | Mock exams, conversation; transcripts for error logs. Cannot judge pronunciation |
| Gemini Live | Built into the Pixel | **Free: Pixel 10 Pro includes 1 year of Google AI Pro** | Extra conversation minutes at no cost |
| ELSA Speak | Android, web | ~US$75–130/yr | Phoneme-level pronunciation scoring |
| Speak | Android | Subscription **[U price in Spain]** | Structured AI speaking drills |
| Langua / Loora | Web, Android | Subscription | Well-reviewed AI conversation apps (2026) |
| Pixel Recorder | Pixel | Free | Record yourself, get an English transcript, paste into Claude |
| ChatGPT voice | Android, Mac | Free ~15 min/day; Plus €23/mo | Alternative voice partner |

## Human feedback

- **italki** / **Preply** (pay per lesson): one exam-focused tutor lesson per week.
- **Cambly** (subscription): on-demand native speakers.
- **Tandem** / **HelloTalk** (free): language exchange.

## Writing and grammar

- **LanguageTool** (Mac app, browser, Android keyboard): free; Premium €59.89/yr. Good for Spanish speakers.
- **Grammarly** (now owned by Superhuman): free; Pro ~US$12/mo yearly.
- **English Grammar in Use (Murphy)**: the app was removed in 2022. Use the book or eBook.

## Courses (supplement only)

- **Duolingo** (Super ~€8/mo, Max ~€180/yr with AI video call), **Babbel**, **Busuu**. Good habit builders; not enough for B2+ alone.

## Mac extras

- **macOS Dictation** (on-device on Apple Silicon): dictate an essay; if it mis-transcribes a word, check that word's pronunciation.
- **Spoken Content** (Accessibility settings): the Mac reads texts aloud.
- **Ollama / LM Studio**: offline local models; useful for privacy, weaker than Claude for feedback.

## Free core stack

Anki + AnkiDroid, Cambridge Dictionary + Ozdic, Write & Improve, Speak & Improve, Gemini Live (free year), Pixel Recorder, Language Reactor, YouGlish, LanguageTool free. Add Claude Pro and one weekly tutor lesson if the budget allows.
