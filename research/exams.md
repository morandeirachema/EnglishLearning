## Which one to pick

- **Job or oposiciones in Spain:** Cambridge B2/C1 (most prestigious). Cheaper and faster: Oxford Test of English. Always check the exam list in the specific *bases* of the convocatoria.
- **Spanish university requirement:** an ACLES-table exam (Cambridge, OTE, Trinity, Linguaskill at a centre). Check your university's own list.
- **Study abroad:** IELTS Academic or TOEFL.
- **Move to UK / Canada / Australia:** IELTS for UKVI or PTE UKVI / IELTS General or PTE Core / IELTS, PTE or C1 Advanced at a centre.
- **Unsure about your level:** a multilevel exam (OTE, Aptis, Linguaskill, ISE Digital) certifies whatever level you reach. Cambridge is riskier near a level boundary.
- **Certificate for life:** Cambridge, OTE, Trinity, Aptis, EOI have no expiry. IELTS/TOEFL/PTE/Duolingo are treated as valid 2 years.

## Recent changes

- Cambridge: test-day photo on Statement of Results from April 2026.
- IELTS: paper-based phased out from mid-2026; One Skill Retake available on computer tests.
- TOEFL iBT: new ~90 min adaptive format and 1–6 scale since 21 Jan 2026.
- Australia accepts more tests since Aug 2025, all at a centre.
